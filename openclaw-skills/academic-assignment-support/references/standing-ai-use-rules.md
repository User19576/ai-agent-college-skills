# Standing AI-use rules for the original learner

The original learner adopted the following AI-use limits for their assignments and will report changes. This does not establish the rules for another learner, even on the same course: check their current brief and instructor directions. The wording is transcribed verbatim from the “Use of AI” section of `5N2434 Skills Demo 1.pdf` (Virtualisation Support 5N5434); line breaks follow the PDF text layout. The original learner's standing permission does not override stricter rules in another assessment brief or from an instructor.

> Use of AI:
> The ETHICAL use of Generative AI tools is permitted for specific functions within
> this
> assessment. These tools may be utilised for the following purposes:
>  Reviewing grammar and spelling
>  Assisting in the preparation of a reference list
>  Providing constructive feedback on written work
> However, the use of Generative AI is strictly prohibited for:
>  Composing reflective content
>  Generate entire essays, reports, or substantial sections of work without significant
> human input.
> Students are strictly prohibited from submitting AI-generated content as their own
> work.
> All assignments will be screened through Turnitin during submission.
> Students are responsible for ensuring that any use of AI in their work complies with
> this
> Cork College of FET- Mallow Campus AI Policy.

Source of quoted wording: `5N2434 Skills Demo 1.pdf`, “Use of AI” section. Cross-assignment applicability is the original learner's separate instruction, not a claim made by that PDF.
